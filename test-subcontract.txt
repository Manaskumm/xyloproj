SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT

1. PARTIES & PROJECT: This Agreement is made by and between BuildCorp General Contractors ("GC") and Apex HVAC Services ("Subcontractor") for the project known as "Highrise Tower A" located at 456 Broad St, Chicago, IL 60601.
2. SCOPE OF WORK: Subcontractor shall provide and install all HVAC systems, ductwork, and exhaust fans as specified in Architectural Drawing Section M-101. Subcontractor is responsible for site cleanup daily. GC will provide hoisting services.
3. SCHEDULE: Subcontractor shall mobilize on site by October 1, 2026. Rough-in must be completed by November 15, 2026. Final inspection and commission must occur by December 20, 2026. Delay penalties of $500 per day will apply for subcontractor-caused delays.
4. FINANCIAL TERMS: The total contract value is $150,000. Payment applications must be submitted by the 25th of each month. GC will withhold a 10% retainage from all progress payments, to be released 30 days after final project acceptance.
5. PAYMENT CLAUSE: Subcontractor agrees that payment by the Owner to the GC is an absolute condition precedent to the GC's obligation to pay the Subcontractor ("Pay-If-Paid").
6. WARRANTY & WARRANTY PERIOD: Subcontractor warrants all materials and workmanship for a period of 2 years from the date of final project acceptance.
7. DISPUTES & DELAYS: Subcontractor must notify GC in writing of any schedule delays or differing site conditions within 3 business days of occurrence, failing which any claim for additional time or cost is waived.
